All post-simulation analyses, including data aggregation, statistical testing, and visualisation, were conducted in R (Version 4.x; R Core Team, 2024) using the tidyverse (Wickham et al., 2019) and ggplot2 (Wickham, 2016) packages. An agent-based model was developed to simulate the dynamics of dietary behaviour change within a social network, grounded in the Reasoned Action Approach (RAA; Fishbein & Ajzen, 2010). The model examines how social influence and habit formation interact to drive population-level transitions between three dietary categories: meat eating, reduced-meat eating, and no-meat eating. The ABM approach was chosen because it allows for the emergence of macro-level patterns from micro-level agent interactions, capturing the non-linear and threshold-dependent nature of behaviour change that is difficult to represent in equation-based models (Railsback & Grimm, 2019).
The NetLogo BehaviourSpace output was processed using custom R scripts to extract, clean, and aggregate the simulation results.
The survey data cannot be shared due to ethical restrictions.
